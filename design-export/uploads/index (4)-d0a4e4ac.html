Meet Dr. Jaydeep Dalvi

A Trusted Urologist in Nagpur

Dr. Jaydeep Dalvi built his career on rigorous training. He completed his MBBS at GMC Nagpur, an MS in General Surgery at VMGMC Solapur, and his MCh in Urology at Sion, Mumbai — one of India’s most respected centres for urological training.

Over the past 11+ years, he has treated more than 10,000 patients across the full spectrum of urology — from common kidney stones to complex cancers, and from paediatric cases to elderly care.

His expertise spans kidney, bladder, prostate, and reconstructive urology, including minimally invasive laser and keyhole procedures, men’s health and andrology, female and paediatric urology, and dialysis support.

His approach is straightforward: an accurate diagnosis first, treatment explained in clear terms, and a plan tailored to each patient’s condition and life.
